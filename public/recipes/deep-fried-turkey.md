# Deep-fried turkey
Equipment: Electric fryer
Source: Your electric fryer's model-specific turkey instructions; USDA FSIS Turkey Basics: Safe Cooking.
Notes: Review draft. Confirm the actual bird weight and the fryer's capacity/displacement limit before setting frying time. The 60-minute warm-up allowance comes from this owner's observed 54-minute warm-up for about 3 gallons of oil at an outdoor ambient temperature of about 45 degrees F. It is editable and is not an all-fryer rule.

## Ingredients
- One fully thawed, unstuffed turkey that fits the fryer's limits
- Frying oil, type and fill level specified by the fryer manual
- Seasoning of choice

## Steps
1. Confirm turkey is fully thawed, unstuffed and dry.
2. Check measured turkey weight and fryer capacity.
3. Season turkey.
4. Prepare electric fryer within its oil-fill/displacement limits.
5. Begin warming oil (55–60 minutes planning allowance).
6. Verify oil is ready at the manual's turkey setting.
7. Lower turkey slowly into oil.
8. Fry turkey until internal temperature reaches 165°F.
9. Remove turkey and drain.
10. Rest turkey (20 minutes); carve and serve when ready.
